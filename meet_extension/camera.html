Allow camera for Focus Alerts

Focus Alerts needs your camera to notice when your eyes close or your attention drifts.

The video is analysed inside Chrome on this computer. It is never uploaded or recorded.

Click Allow in Chrome's camera prompt at the top of this window.

The camera is blocked for Focus Alerts. To allow it:

  1. Click the camera icon (or the tune icon) at the left of the address bar.
  2. Set Camera to Allow.
  3. Click Try again.
